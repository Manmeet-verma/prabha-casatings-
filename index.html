About Our Company

Welcome to Makkah Steel Alloys

Established in 1998, Makkah Steel Alloys is a renowned name in the manufacturing and trading of premium quality industrial rolls and steel components. Located in Maler Kotla, Punjab, we have been serving the flour milling, paper manufacturing, and rolling mill industries with unwavering commitment to quality.

Under the visionary leadership of Mr. A. Rashid (Owner), we have built a reputation for delivering exceptional products that meet the highest industry standards. Our extensive range includes Chilled Rolls, S.G. Iron Rolls, Adamite Rolls, and specialized rolls for various industrial applications.
